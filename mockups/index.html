Review notes
Features and research

Home

One to six apps, clock, and date. Rename and reorder apps.

Search

Local app search. Tap or press Enter to open.

Appearance

Font, text size, alignment, and light or black themes.

Wallpaper

Optional daily shuffle. Skip or keep an image. Adjustable dimming.

Pause

Optional three-second delay for selected apps opened through Stil.

  • Olauncher: text-first home. Its guide documents hidden controls. Stil uses visible settings.
  • Niagara: list navigation. KISS: search and speed.
  • Before: notification filtering, deferred for Stil.
  • minimalist phone: launch delays. Stil's pause needs user testing.
  • Android private space needs separate handling. Only personal apps appear here.
